# Campaign asset

`headset-campaign.png` is the user's supplied **Untitled design.png** headset cutout. Only fully transparent outside margins were trimmed; the product pixels and transparency were retained. It is bundled for offline banner rendering and is independent of administrator-uploaded catalog photos.

The supplied image depicts a wired headset. Banner links browse/search collections; the image is not substituted for the existing Wireless headphones product photo or advertised as that product's exact appearance.

`login-pc-campaign.jpg` is the user's supplied **login.jpg** PC photo (848×1264). The original JPEG pixels are retained. The account campaign blends its deep-black photo background into the charcoal/orange artwork with JavaFX `LIGHTEN`, preserving the model's dark detail while allowing the rings and glow to show through. The login photo is campaign artwork, not a seeded catalog item or a specification claim.

`login-pc-campaign.png` is the previous login artwork and is retained as an unused project asset; the account campaign now loads `login-pc-campaign.jpg`.
